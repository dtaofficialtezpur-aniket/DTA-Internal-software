1. How online selling works at DTA

All selling is done online: phone, WhatsApp, email and video calls. You visit a client only when the client calls us and asks for a visit. This lets one person serve customers across a whole state — and lets DTA reach customers anywhere in India.

1 · AttractBe seen where customers search
2 · CaptureGet name and number
3 · Respond fastWithin 15 minutes
4 · QualifyUnderstand the need
5 · Online demoScreen-share, 25 min
6 · CloseOffer, payment, start

Five rules of online selling

  1. Speed wins. The first person to reply properly usually gets the customer. Reply to every enquiry within 15 minutes in working hours.
  2. Every enquiry goes into the DTA Sales app the same day, with where it came from (write the source in the lead’s Notes, e.g. “Source: Google / WhatsApp / LinkedIn / Referral”).
  3. Trust must be built online. Customers can’t see your shop — so show proof: real examples, a clear profile, a professional message and a live demo.
  4. Keep it personal. Use their name, language and business details. No copy-paste blasts.
  5. Follow up. Most online enquiries don’t buy on the first chat. Follow up on a schedule.

When does a visit happen?

Only when a client calls us and asks for it. If a client wants a visit: log it in the app, tell your manager, and agree the date, place and who will attend. Do not offer visits yourself to cold leads — offer an online demo.

Which enquiries are mine?

Handle enquiries from your state. If an enquiry comes from another state, pass it to that state’s employee (your manager will tell you the routing rule) — and reply to the customer meanwhile so they don’t wait. Speak in the customer’s language wherever you can.

4. When an enquiry arrives

The 15-minute rule: reply to every new enquiry within 15 minutes in working hours. Outside working hours, an instant auto-reply, then a real reply first thing in the morning.

The 5 steps

StepWhat to do
1 · AcknowledgeReply on the same channel they used, with their name. Say who you are and that you’ll help.
2 · CallCall within the hour (if they gave a number). A voice builds trust faster than text. If they don’t answer, send a WhatsApp: “I tried calling — what time suits you?”
3 · QualifyAsk 4–6 questions: what business, what problem, what they use today, who decides, how soon, rough budget.
4 · Book the demoFix a date and time for an online demo with the decision-maker. Send the meeting link and a calendar reminder.
5 · RecordAdd to the DTA Sales app with state, city, product type, source in Notes, expected value and the next follow-up date.

Scripts

WhatsApp reply (first message): “Namaste ___ ji, thank you for contacting DTA. I’m ___ . I saw you’re looking for ___ . May I call you in the next 10 minutes to understand what you need?”
Inbound call: “Namaste, DTA, this is ___ . How can I help you?” … [listen] … “Tell me a little about your business, so I suggest the right thing.” … [questions] … “Let me show you how this works on a short video call — are you free today at 4 or tomorrow at 11?”
After-hours auto-reply: “Thank you for contacting DTA! Our team will reply first thing in the morning (9:30 am – 6 pm, Mon–Sat). To help us serve you faster, please tell us your business type and what you need.”
Missed call: “Hello ___ ji, sorry we missed your call. This is ___ from DTA. When is a good time to speak today?”

Tip: always find out the business type and state (so the right employee and product are used), the problem, what they use now, who decides, and when they want to start.

Building trust when they can’t meet you

  • Show you are real: introduce DTA, your name and state, company profile, website and Google listing.
  • Show proof: real examples, screenshots and short testimonials approved by manager; offer to connect them with a similar client if the client agrees.
  • Be clear in writing: what is included, price, start date, support — in a short PDF or message.
  • Be reliable: join on time, send what you promised the same day.

6. Closing, follow-up and tracking

Closing online

  1. After the demo, send a summary the same day: problem → solution → price/plan → start date.
  2. Ask: “Shall we start on [date]?” or offer a choice between approved plans.
  3. Follow your manager’s payment and agreement process (online payment link / bank transfer; written confirmation). Never share personal bank details unless DTA’s process says so.
  4. In the app, mark the lead Won with the final deal value; hand over to the DTA team for setup.
  5. Call after 7 days (“Is everything working?”) and 30 days (“What has improved?”). Ask for a Google review and a referral.
